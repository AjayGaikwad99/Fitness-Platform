About GYM

Our Story

What began as a small garage operation in 2018 has blossomed into Vasai’s premier fitness destination. Founded by athletes for athletes, GYM’s mission is to democratize world-class coaching and facilities.

Mission & Vision

We are here to empower every person, regardless of background or experience, to achieve their health goals. Through evidence-based training, personalized nutrition, and a supportive community, we envision a fitter, happier world.

Facilities

  • Free-weight area with dumbbells up to 100kg & Olympic platforms
  • Full cardio suite: treadmills, ellipticals, rowers & spin bikes
  • Functional zone: battle ropes, kettlebells, medicine balls & rigs
  • Dedicated stretching & mobility studios
  • On-site smoothie bar offering protein blends & recovery snacks

Community & Events

From monthly transformation challenges to weekend workshops on mobility, we keep our members engaged and motivated. Join group classes, bootcamps, or one-on-one coaching sessions.
